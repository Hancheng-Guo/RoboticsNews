RoboColosseum 在分布式实机网络中评测机器人策略

RoboColosseum: Evaluating Physical Intelligence at Scale In-The-Wild

作者：Tan Sang Nguyen（第一作者）、Yi Ru Wang、Shailesh Shailesh、Gang Yang、Jiamo Hu、Jianuo Jiang、Haoquan Fang、Zhongzheng Ren、Zhongyi Luo、Ian Chong、Aaron Tung、Santiago Pravisani、Minghui Lin、Yao Mo、Ishneet Singh、Benjamin Tee、Harold Soh、Siddhartha Srinivasa、Dieter Fox、David Hsu、Haoming Song、Maxime Ellerbach、Thomas Wolf、Pepijn Kooijmans、Ruijie He、Michael Cho、Jiafei Duan（通讯作者）
第一作者单位：National University of Singapore，新加坡
通讯作者单位：Jiafei Duan：National University of Singapore，新加坡；Singapore Embodied AI Centre，新加坡

论文摘要原文 / Original abstract

General-purpose robot policies must work across robots, tasks, and environments, yet the variation that makes physical trials informative also makes them difficult to reproduce. We introduce RoboColosseum, a distributed evaluation framework that combines unified policy serving and standardized protocols across a network of locally operated robots in diverse environments. Our initial study evaluates five vision-language-action (VLA) policies and three frontier AI agents across four embodiments (SO-101, Franka, Bimanual-YAM, and G1 Humanoid). The Open Track compares models without task-specific adaptation in blinded, pairwise trials under matched local conditions. The Fine-tune Track measures how well VLA policies adapt to 20 tasks across all four embodiments when each policy receives the same 100 demonstrations per task. Across 5,699 pairwise Open Track comparisons at 124 sites, frontier agents rival specialized VLA policies, while all systems struggle to complete open-world tasks reliably. After fine-tuning, GR00T N1.7 leads on SO-101 and pi0.5 leads on Franka and Bimanual-YAM. Policy rankings show weak agreement across embodiment-specific task suites (Kendall W = 0.24). Open Track rankings poorly predict relative performance after fine-tuning, with substantial rank reversals on all three embodiments shared between tracks. Measuring partial progress reveals differences that task success alone misses. On the G1 Humanoid, no policy completes any of the five tasks, yet average progress ranges from 13.3% to 23.2% across policies. Open Track rankings stabilize as trials accumulate, and active matchmaking achieves comparable ranking agreement with substantially fewer physical trials than uniform sampling. These results show how distributed physical evaluation can reveal where robot policies generalize, how well they adapt to new tasks, and how far they progress on tasks they fail to complete.

Pixel 联合设计移动操作机器人的硬件、数据采集与控制

Pixel: A Co-Designed Full-Stack Robot System for Learning General-Purpose Mobile Manipulation

作者：Tony Tao（第一作者）、Jason Jingzhou Liu、Ruslan Salakhutdinov、Deepak Pathak
第一作者单位：Carnegie Mellon University，美国
通讯作者单位：论文未注明通讯作者

论文摘要原文 / Original abstract

General-purpose mobile manipulation requires a combination of capable robot hardware, scalable demonstration collection, expressive whole-body control, and effective policy learning. Yet these components are often developed independently, creating mismatches that limit the capabilities of the complete system. We present Pixel, a co-designed full-stack system for learning general-purpose mobile manipulation. Pixel combines a low-cost wheeled humanoid with human-aligned kinematics and compliant grippers, multiple teleoperation and robot-free human data-collection interfaces, a unified whole-body controller, and a common policy learning pipeline across human and robot demonstrations. A hierarchical learning stack further enables language-conditioned visuomotor control and long-horizon task execution. We evaluate the system across hardware, data collection, and policy learning, and demonstrate mobile-manipulation behaviors requiring dexterity, active perception, navigation, and whole-body coordination. We hope that Pixel provides an integrated and extensible foundation for studying how robot hardware, data, control, and learning can be designed together for robot learning. BeyondContact 在仿真中训练吹气与推吹混合操控

BeyondContact: A Reinforcement Learning Framework for Airflow-Based Non-Contact Manipulation

作者：Peiyu Yang（第一作者）、Mohamad S. Moudallal、Antonio Langella、Giulio Turrisi、Victor Barasuol、Claudio Semini
第一作者单位：Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia，意大利
通讯作者单位：论文未注明通讯作者

论文摘要原文 / Original abstract

Contact-based robotic manipulation is constrained by end-effector reachability and the mechanical properties of objects. Airflow can exert forces without direct robot-object contact, enabling manipulation of objects beyond the reach or when direct contact is undesirable. However, the lack of suitable simulation has hindered the application of reinforcement learning to airflow-based manipulation, limiting the broader adoption of this mechanism compared with contact-based methods. To address this gap, we developed a robot- and task-agnostic airflow extension for Isaac Lab that supports massively parallel training. The extension uses experimentally identified airflow models to compute the forces and torques exerted on objects. Based on this extension, we train a hybrid push-blow (HPB) policy that combines contact-based pushing with airflow manipulation and a rectangular reorientation (RR) policy that controls a cuboid's heading using airflow alone. To enable physical execution, we design a lightweight, high-speed blower that coexists with a gripper. In zero-shot real-world deployment, HPB achieves an 80% success rate over 20 trials, while RR achieves a mean absolute heading error of 2.6 degrees across four target headings. The project materials are available at this https URL.

示范学习策略通过交互在线估计物体的潜在物理属性

Online Adaptation to Latent Physical Properties for Learning from Demonstration

作者：Kotaro Kajitsuka（第一作者）、Masaki Murooka、Fumio Kanehiro
第一作者单位：University of Tsukuba，日本；CNRS-AIST JRL, National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology，日本
通讯作者单位：论文未注明通讯作者

论文摘要原文 / Original abstract

Manipulating visually identical objects with different physical properties requires adapting actions based on information acquired through interaction. We address the problem of learning from demonstrations how to adapt manipulation actions, such as regrasping and changing contact locations, based on physical-property information acquired during interaction. To this end, we propose a learning-from-demonstration method that learns latent representations of physical properties needed to predict object responses and conditions a policy on online estimates of these representations. A dynamics model and object-specific latent representations are jointly learned from demonstrations that include behaviors adapting to the objects' physical properties, and the learned representations are then used to condition an imitation policy. During execution, the dynamics model and policy weights remain fixed, while the latent representation is updated based on the discrepancy between predicted and actual observations, allowing interaction with the object to inform action generation. Real-world and simulation experiments with objects whose centers of mass differ suggest that online adaptation through latent representations of physical properties can improve task success rates. However, in simulation, success rates on center-of-mass locations absent from the demonstrations remained around 30%, indicating limited adaptation to unseen physical properties. 用地面高度估计剔除反光地面的虚假里程计特征

Reflection-Robust Stereo Visual-Inertial Odometry via Kalman-Filtered Ground-Height Estimation

作者：Uihyun Hwang（第一作者）、Sungjae Shin、Dongjae Kim、Hyun Myung（通讯作者）
第一作者单位：Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology，韩国
通讯作者单位：Hyun Myung：Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology，韩国

论文摘要原文 / Original abstract

Visual-inertial odometry relies on visual features that correspond to 3D points with consistent projections across sequential frames. In reflective indoor-floor environments, however, specular floor patterns can be detected and triangulated as virtual features below the physical ground plane, producing unreliable feature associations and inconsistent visual residuals. This paper proposes a lightweight reflection-aware front-end for feature-based stereo VIO. Rather than suppressing all floor-region features, the proposed method estimates the ground height in real time and rejects only feature candidates whose inferred 3D positions are below the estimated ground height. A floor-region mask restricts the validation process to reflection-prone regions, while stereo height uncertainty and pose uncertainty are used to construct frame-level ground-height measurements. A scalar Kalman filter with Normalized Innovation Squared (NIS) gating then provides a stable ground-height estimate, and an uncertainty-aware safety margin is used for selective below-ground virtual feature rejection. Experiments on real reflective-floor indoor sequences show that the proposed front-end generally improves trajectory accuracy in reflection-dominant environments while maintaining accurate ground-height estimates and recovering from actual ground-height changes.

视觉惯性里程计依靠与三维点对应、且在连续帧中投影一致的视觉特征。然而，在室内反光地面环境中，地面的镜面反射图案可能被检测并三角化为真实地面下方的虚拟特征，导致特征关联不可靠、视觉残差不一致。本文提出一种用于基于特征的双目视觉惯性里程计的轻量抗反射前端。该方法实时估计地面高度，仅拒绝推断三维位置低于估计地面高度的候选特征，并保留其他地面区域特征。地面区域掩码将验证限制在易反光区域，利用双目高度不确定性和位姿不确定性构造逐帧地面高度测量。随后，采用归一化新息平方（NIS）门控的标量卡尔曼滤波器给出稳定的地面高度估计，并利用考虑不确定性的安全裕量选择性地剔除地面下方的虚拟特征。在真实室内反光地面序列上的实验表明，在反射占主导的环境中，该前端通常能提高轨迹精度，同时保持准确的地面高度估计，并能在实际地面高度改变后恢复。

LMGN 将四足机器人抓取前的接近和操作纳入同一策略

LMGN: A Unified Policy Learning Model for Last-Mile Grasp-Navigation

作者：Xuenmin Hu（第一作者）、Qingxue Qian、Wei Luo、Benyi Ma、Bo Tang、Long Chen（通讯作者）
第一作者单位：Hubei University，中国
通讯作者单位：Long Chen：Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences，中国

论文摘要原文 / Original abstract

Legged robots equipped with manipulators have shown significant potential in the fields of daily life and industry in complex environments. However, existing methods mainly focus on global navigation or manipulation independently, while overlooking the last-mile navigation problem, where the robot must jointly determine the base placement, approach direction, and grasp execution to achieve reliable object grasping, and different manipulation stages require distinct control behaviors. To address these challenges, we propose Last-Mile Grasp-Navigation (LMGN), a unified policy learning model for Quadruped robot loco-manipulation. An Explicit Distance and Directional Reachability (ED-DR) representation is first introduced to explicitly represent the features of grasp-compatible base placement and approach direction. We then develop a semantically aligned Hierarchical Mixture-of-Experts (HMoE) policy that models stage-aware behaviors across the stages of base adjustment, pre-grasp alignment and grasp execution, and object lift. Finally, a new optimization method that jointly aligns navigation and manipulation, stage-dependent expert routing, and action-level consistency is proposed to optimize the HMoE policy. Extensive experiments on the Habitat and Isaac Gym simulators demonstrate that LMGN consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods, denoting that integrating grasp-aware reachability with stage-aware policy learning significantly improves the success rates of last-mile navigation and target grasp for robots.

摘要中文翻译

带机械臂的足式机器人在复杂环境中的日常生活和工业应用中展现出显著潜力。然而，现有方法主要分别关注全局导航或操作，忽视了最后一段导航问题。为可靠抓取物体，机器人必须联合确定底座位置、接近方向和抓取执行方式，不同的操作阶段也需要不同的控制行为。为解决这些问题，我们提出最后一段抓取导航（LMGN），一种面向四足机器人移动操作的统一策略学习模型。首先引入显式距离与方向可达性（ED-DR）表征，显式描述与抓取兼容的底座位置和接近方向特征。随后，我们开发语义对齐的分层混合专家（HMoE）策略，为底座调整、抓取前对齐与抓取执行、物体抬升等阶段建模相应行为。最后，我们提出新的优化方法，联合对齐导航与操作、依阶段路由专家以及动作层面的一致性，以优化 HMoE 策略。在 Habitat 和 Isaac Gym 仿真器中的广泛实验显示，LMGN 持续优于现有先进方法，表明将考虑抓取的可达性与依阶段的策略学习结合，可显著提高机器人最后一段导航和目标抓取的成功率。
