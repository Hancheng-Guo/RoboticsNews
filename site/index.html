GXO 与 Agility 扩大 Digit 仓储机器人协议，新增两处美国站点

GXO and Agility agree to extend Digit to two additional U.S. sites

GXO 与 Agility 公告，Digit 将进入北卡罗来纳州 Statesville 和田纳西州 Lebanon 的 GXO 站点，预计 2027 年初开始部署 Digit 4，随后转向 Digit 5。GXO 表示其佐治亚州站点自 2024 年商业协议以来已搬运超过 10 万个料箱；新站点的实际运行尚待观察。

GXO and Agility agreed to add Digit to GXO sites in Statesville, North Carolina, and Lebanon, Tennessee. Deployments are expected to start with Digit 4 in early 2027 and later transition to Digit 5. GXO reports more than 100,000 totes moved at its Georgia site since the 2024 commercial agreement; operation at the new sites has yet to begin.

意义：由现有客户宣布跨站点扩展，为人形机器人在仓储任务中的商业复用提供后续观察点。

局限：新站点是协议和计划，尚非已完成部署；料箱数量为合作双方披露，未见独立审计。

KDDI 公布 ELYZA Robotics 业务及机器人研发投入计划

KDDI announces ELYZA Robotics and its physical AI investment plan

KDDI 与 ELYZA 于 10 月 27 日首次公告，新公司 ELYZA Robotics 已在 10 月 1 日开展业务。KDDI 计划到 2030 财年为物理 AI 研发投入 300 亿日元、组建 300 人以上团队，并以部署超过 1000 台机器人为目标；均属于未来计划，公告未给出已部署数量。

KDDI and ELYZA announced on October 27 that ELYZA Robotics had begun operations on October 1. KDDI plans to invest ¥30 billion in physical AI R&D and build a team of more than 300 by fiscal 2030, targeting deployment of more than 1,000 robots. These are future targets; the release does not report deployments already achieved.

意义：把模型研发、硬件和现场部署放在同一业务计划中，值得持续追踪实际产品与部署进展。

局限：公告日晚于业务开始日；投资、团队和部署数字均为未来目标，未披露已投入或已部署规模。

The company presents generated interactive worlds as a potential environment for robot and agent training, without reporting validated robot policy transfer or hardware gains.

Luxonis 发布 OAK 4 FFC 多摄像头视觉计算板

Luxonis releases the OAK 4 FFC multi-camera vision computer

Open Robotics 联盟 10 月 27 日公告 Luxonis 发布 OAK 4 FFC，并升级为 OSRA Silver 会员。官方称该 70 × 53 毫米板卡可连接最多五个摄像头，处理同步、深度和设备端 AI；产品页和参考设计已公开，实际机器人应用性能仍需单独测试。

The Open Robotics Alliance announced on October 27 that Luxonis released OAK 4 FFC and upgraded to OSRA Silver membership. It says the 70 × 53 mm board supports up to five cameras for synchronization, depth and on-device AI. The product page and reference design are available; performance in specific robots still needs testing.

意义：为多视角机器人感知系统提供紧凑的集成硬件选项。

局限：规格来自发布方；未见第三方对延迟、功耗或复杂场景精度的测试。

ARX and ROBONEERS report initial GEREON–SABER integration tests

ARX Robotics 10 月 29 日称，与 ROBONEERS 完成 SABER 模块和 GEREON 无人地面平台的初步集成测试；两家公司计划年底前完成 M2 与 MK 19 (M) 配置集成。公告未提供测试协议、连续可靠性数据或本组合的已交付记录。

意义：模块化地面平台的互操作性是特种机器人系统集成的重要进展。

Agility 与 Churchill XI 预计交易相关融资总额约 4.65 亿美元

Agility and Churchill XI expect about $465 million in gross transaction proceeds

双方 10 月 30 日公告，拟议业务合并及配套融资的预计总筹资约 4.65 亿美元，包含信托账户约 1.64 亿、PIPE 承诺约 2.01 亿和此前已收到的 1 亿美元。交易仍须股东批准和满足交割条件，预计于 11 月 4 日完成；数字为交割前预计值，不是已实现营收。

Agility and Churchill XI said on October 30 that expected gross proceeds from the proposed combination and related financing were about $465 million: roughly $164 million from the trust, $201 million of PIPE commitments and $100 million already received. Closing remained subject to shareholder approval and other conditions, with November 4 targeted. The figure is projected transaction funding, not earned revenue.

意义：潜在融资影响人形机器人产能和客户扩展，但须与实际交割及运营收入区分。

局限：公告时交易尚未交割；本条不将预期融资或订单承诺计为已到账收入。

Hunan humanoid robotics semifinal uses on-site practical tasks

Rendezvous Robotics 宣布获 2000 万美元种子轮，用于在轨自主组装飞行演示

Rendezvous Robotics announces $20 million seed round for orbital self-assembly

Rendezvous Robotics 10 月 28 日宣布完成由 MaC Venture Capital 和 Toyota Ventures 领投的 2000 万美元种子轮。公司正地面测试可自主移动、对准和连接的模块化航天器单元，并计划 2028 年开展七块单元的自由飞行组装与重新配置演示；该任务尚未在轨完成。

Rendezvous Robotics announced a $20 million seed round led by MaC Venture Capital and Toyota Ventures. It is ground-testing modular spacecraft tiles for autonomous movement, alignment and connection, and plans a seven-tile free-flight assembly and reconfiguration demonstration in 2028. The orbital mission has not occurred.

意义：资金支持空间自主组装从受控演示向自由飞行验证迈进。

局限：融资与时间表来自公司公告；2028 年任务属于计划，不能视为已验证的在轨能力。
